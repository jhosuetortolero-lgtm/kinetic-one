Overview

KINETIC ONE is a closed-back wireless headphone built around a single idea: remove everything that stands between you and the recording.

Drivers, silicon and frame were designed together, by one team, in one building in Copenhagen. Nothing was sourced off the shelf and adapted to fit.

No logo on the cups. No gloss. No buttons you can see. Titanium, knit and memory foam, assembled so that every part can be replaced.

  • 2devices

    Stays connected to your laptop and your phone at the same time. A call takes over from music automatically.
